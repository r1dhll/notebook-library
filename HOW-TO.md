# How to use this

Ask it to explain a topic. Ask it to teach you something. Ask it to walk you through a problem, step by step. Ask it to help you see the pattern in a hard problem.

If you do not get it, or you want to learn it deeply, ask again. One level at a time.

1. Tell it to teach it as if you are in primary school.
2. Then at middle school.
3. Then at the level you are at now.

When a reply is worth keeping, click Save as note. It stays in the right sidebar.

For the rest, use Studio on the right.

- An audio overview of that topic. Listen on the way, or when you do not have time to sit and study.
- A mind map for a chapter, so you can see how the topic fits.
- Flashcards for formulas and things you need to remember.
- A report, or study material made the way you want. A slide deck downloads as a PDF. A written guide opens as a document you can print. Say which chapter, which formulas, and which questions.

Edit the custom instructions if you want every answer shaped your way.

Once you understand the topic, ask it for questions. Get good by doing them. Prefer the NCERT questions and the sample papers in this notebook.
